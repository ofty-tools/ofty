Clinical approach

How these tools think

Stage-based thinking Risk modifier awareness Always individualised Progression-aware

Glaucoma management is fundamentally about weighing risk against burden of treatment for each individual patient. These tools are built around that principle — they do not produce a single answer, but a structured, evidence-referenced starting point for clinical reasoning.

IOP remains the primary modifiable risk factor, but it cannot be read in isolation. Corneal thickness, optic nerve appearance, visual field trajectory, age, and systemic factors all shape what a given pressure means for a given patient. The tools here are designed to surface those contextual layers quickly and transparently.

Importantly, CCT is treated here as a risk and measurement-reliability modifier — not as a basis for a numeric IOP "correction." This aligns with the current consensus from EGS and other major guideline bodies, and with the evidence that no validated correction formula exists for clinical use.

All outputs must be reassessed in light of disease progression, new clinical findings, and the complete picture of the individual patient. These tools assist judgment — they do not replace it.

Disclaimer

OFTY tools provide decision support only and do not constitute medical advice. All clinical decisions must be individualised by a qualified clinician.

  • These tools are for decision support only. They do not replace clinical examination, professional judgment, or individualised patient assessment.
  • All outputs must be interpreted in the full clinical context of each patient.
  • CCT values are used as a contextual risk factor — they do not imply a numeric IOP correction, in line with current evidence and major guideline recommendations (EGS, AAO).
  • Clinical situations evolve. Always reassess in light of disease progression, new findings, and the overall clinical picture.
  • OFTY does not store patient data and is not a certified medical device.
