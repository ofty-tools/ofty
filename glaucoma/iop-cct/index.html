Clinical note: Thin CCT is an independent risk factor for glaucoma development (OHTS). Use this adjusted estimate for risk stratification — not as a standalone diagnostic threshold. Corneal hysteresis and other biomechanical properties are not reflected in this model.

This tool provides an evidence-informed estimate. It must not replace clinical judgment.

OFTY Tool #1 applies a simplified linear correction to Goldmann-measured IOP based on central corneal thickness (CCT), using a reference value of 540 µm and a correction factor of 1 mmHg per 25 µm deviation. This adjustment is a theoretical estimate grounded in published epidemiological models.

Individual corneal biomechanics — including hysteresis, rigidity, and structural properties — are not accounted for by this model. Additional patient-specific risk factors may substantially alter clinical interpretation.

The clinician retains full responsibility for all diagnostic and therapeutic decisions. This tool is intended as a decision-support aid, consistent with current glaucoma guidelines, and is not a substitute for clinical evaluation. Not intended for patient self-use.

  • AAO Preferred Practice Pattern — Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma
