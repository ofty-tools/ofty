Conceptual Framework

OFTY Method

The OFTY framework provides structured interpretation of clinical data used in the longitudinal monitoring of ophthalmic disease. Its purpose is not to generate decisions, but to organise heterogeneous clinical information into coherent, domain-specific interpretative outputs that support — rather than substitute — clinical reasoning.

Core Philosophy

Chronic ophthalmic disease management requires the concurrent interpretation of parameters that evolve at different rates, carry different degrees of measurement uncertainty, and may follow discordant trajectories over time. Reliance on isolated values or informal heuristics can introduce unnecessary variability into clinical interpretation.

The central premise of the OFTY method is that clinical data becomes more interpretable when it is contextualised systematically, with explicit acknowledgment of what each parameter can and cannot indicate at a given point in time.

Methodological Principles

The method is grounded in several interdependent principles. Clinical domains — pressure-related, structural, and functional — are analysed independently rather than collapsed into composite indices, preserving the interpretative value of domain-specific signals, including discordant patterns. Interpretation is oriented toward longitudinal trends rather than single observations, reflecting the chronic nature of the conditions addressed.

All outputs are weighted by the reliability of the underlying data: measurement quality, series length, and internal consistency directly influence interpretative confidence and output classification. Where data are insufficient or ambiguous, the system generates explicit statements of limited assessability rather than forced classifications. Uncertainty is not suppressed; it is reported as a structural component of the interpretative output.

Descriptive Positioning

OFTY generates descriptive outputs. It does not prescribe management decisions or replace clinical judgment. The clinician retains full interpretative authority. The role of the framework is to structure the available evidence into a transparent analytical summary that can be evaluated, challenged, and contextualised within the individual patient's clinical history.

Modular Architecture

The framework is organised as a set of independent analytical domains, each addressing a distinct clinical dimension. Outputs from individual domains may be considered collectively, but their analytical independence is preserved by design. This architecture helps ensure that uncertainty in one domain does not automatically propagate into others, and that each interpretative output remains traceable to its methodological basis.

Generalizability

The underlying principles — domain separation, longitudinal analysis, reliability-weighted interpretation, and explicit uncertainty — are not specific to any single condition. The method is designed for extensibility across ophthalmic subspecialties in which structured longitudinal monitoring is clinically relevant.

OFTY functions as a structured interpretative layer within the clinical reasoning process: one that makes the assumptions of interpretation explicit, the limitations of available data transparent, and the grounds for clinical judgment clearer.
