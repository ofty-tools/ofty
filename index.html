Clinical framework

OFTY Glaucoma Suite

A modular clinical reasoning framework for structured interpretation of glaucoma monitoring parameters — covering IOP contextualization, individualized target pressure estimation, functional and structural progression analysis, and integrated cross-module synthesis.

M1 v1.0

IOP + CCT Contextualizer

Computes a CCT-adjusted IOP estimate using a simplified linear correction model and places it in clinical context — providing reliability flags and interpretative cautions rather than a definitive corrected value. Aligned with EGS and AAO guidelines.

Open tool
M2 v1.0

Target IOP Calculator

Estimates a personalised target IOP range by integrating glaucoma type, disease stage, corneal thickness category, and progression status. Works with or without a documented baseline pressure, adapting its estimation logic accordingly.

Open tool
M3 v1.0

Functional Progression Analysis

Analyses longitudinal visual field MD slope using linear regression and a multi-gate rule-based algorithm — generating one of four structured outcomes: Stable, Suspicious trend, Progressing, or Not assessable.

Open tool
M4 v1.0

Structural Progression Analysis

Analyses longitudinal RNFL thinning rates from OCT data using linear regression and a hierarchical rule cascade — generating one of five structured outcomes: Stable, Suspicious trend, Fast progression, Signal limited, or Not assessable.

Open tool
M5 Orchestrator v1.0

Integrated Clinical Synthesis

Combines the outputs of M1–M4 into a unified structural–functional interpretation contextualised by pressure status. Classifies six clinical patterns — including concordant progression and structural-functional discordance — and applies confidence adjustments for floor effects, signal limitations, and incomplete data.

About OFTY

A growing ecosystem of clinical tools

OFTY began as a focused effort to build the kind of lightweight, evidence-grounded decision-support tools that are genuinely useful during a real consultation. The first suite addresses glaucoma management — an area where numbers are central but context is everything.

The goal is to expand progressively into other ophthalmic subspecialties, always with the same principles: transparent reasoning, appropriate acknowledgment of uncertainty, and strict respect for the clinician's role. OFTY is created and maintained by Andrea Ragosta, MD, FEBO.

Disclaimer

OFTY tools provide decision support only and do not constitute medical advice. All clinical decisions must be individualised by a qualified clinician.

  • These tools are for decision support only. They do not replace clinical examination, professional judgment, or individualised patient assessment.
  • All outputs must be interpreted in the full clinical context of each patient.
  • CCT values are used to compute a contextualised IOP estimate and to flag tonometric reliability — the adjusted value is presented with explicit interpretative cautions, not as a definitive corrected pressure, in line with current evidence and major guideline recommendations (EGS, AAO).
  • Clinical situations evolve. Always reassess in light of disease progression, new findings, and the overall clinical picture.
  • OFTY does not store patient data and is not a certified medical device.
