Andrea Ragosta MD, FEBO

Ophthalmologist, Glaucoma & Anterior Segment

Biography

Andrea Ragosta is a board-certified ophthalmologist and surgeon specialising in glaucoma and anterior segment disease. He holds the Fellow of the European Board of Ophthalmology (FEBO) qualification.

He has developed his clinical and surgical experience across hospital-based and private settings, including university-affiliated institutions. His practice covers the full range of glaucoma care — from diagnosis and risk stratification to medical management and surgery — alongside anterior segment procedures including cataract extraction.

He is the founder of OFTY, a clinical decision-support initiative providing structured, evidence-referenced tools for ophthalmic workflows.

Clinical Focus

  • Glaucoma diagnosis and management
  • Surgical glaucoma techniques
  • Cataract surgery
  • Clinical workflow optimisation

About OFTY

OFTY is a clinical decision-support initiative designed to provide structured, evidence-informed starting points for ophthalmology workflows. Tools are developed with reference to current guideline recommendations — including those of the European Glaucoma Society (EGS) and the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) — and are intended for use by qualified clinicians as an adjunct to, not a substitute for, clinical judgment.

The initiative currently focuses on glaucoma management and is designed to expand progressively into other ophthalmic subspecialties.
